Source: Elabe, 14-16 October 2026. All voters: 54% certain of their choice.

Turnout and undecided voters

Ifop-Fiducial (17-19 October 2026) · certainty of voting in the first round: completely certain 77.6% · almost certain 15.6% · very likely 5.5% · not certain 1.3%.

Ipsos bva-CESI (3-9 September 2026) · intention to vote in the first round (rating from 0 to 10): certain to vote (10/10) 67% · almost certain (9/10) 12% · potential abstainers (6 to 8) 12% · probable abstainers (0 to 5) 9%. Among voters certain to vote, 8% to 12% do not express a voting intention according to the tested assumption.

Six months out, turnout remains the main unknown: voting intentions only cover voters who state a choice.
